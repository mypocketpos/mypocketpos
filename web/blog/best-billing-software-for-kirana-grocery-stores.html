Best Billing Software for Kirana & Grocery Stores in India (2026)

Updated 31 July 2026 · 6 min read

A kirana counter lives and dies by speed. The right billing software should make a sale faster than a calculator and a notebook ever could — while quietly keeping your stock, taxes and customer dues in order.

What a grocery store actually needs from a POS

Big enterprise POS suites are built for supermarkets with dedicated cashiers. A neighbourhood kirana or grocery store needs something leaner. These are the features that pay for themselves within a week:

  • Fast item entry — search by name, product code or barcode, and add to the cart in one tap.
  • Barcode support — scan with the phone camera or a cheap USB/Bluetooth scanner. For loose or local items, generate your own barcode.
  • GST-ready invoices — tax totals calculated automatically, so every bill is compliant without manual maths.
  • Stock tracking — know what's running low before a customer asks for it.
  • Udhar / credit ledger — record dues against a customer's mobile number instead of a paper diary.
  • Works offline — the counter should never stop because the Wi-Fi did.

Phone-first beats a full computer setup

You don't need a desktop, a receipt server and a wiring job to bill well. A modern app runs on an ordinary Android phone or tablet. That means a lower upfront cost, no IT headaches, and the ability to bill from anywhere in the shop — even at the shelf while helping a customer.

Rule of thumb: if adding an item to a bill takes more than two taps, the software is slowing you down. Speed at the counter is the single biggest driver of a good POS.

Don't forget the back office

Billing is only half the job. Over a month, the same tool should quietly answer questions like: which products sell fastest, what's my daily collection, how much udhar is outstanding, and what needs reordering. Look for simple daily sales reports and low-stock alerts rather than dashboards you'll never open.

Multi-counter and multi-store growth

If you run more than one billing counter — or plan to open a second shop — pick software that supports multiple POS counters and per-location stock from day one. Migrating later is painful; starting on the right base is free. See our guide to multi-warehouse inventory management.
