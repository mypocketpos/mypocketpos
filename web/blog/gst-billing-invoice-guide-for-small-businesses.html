GST Billing & Invoicing: A Simple Guide for Small Retailers

Updated 31 July 2026 · 6 min read

GST doesn't have to mean spreadsheets and headaches. Once you understand what a compliant invoice needs, the right billing software does the maths for every sale — automatically.

This is a plain-language overview for shop owners, not tax advice. For your specific registration and filing obligations, check with a qualified accountant or the official GST portal.

What a GST invoice must contain

A tax invoice typically includes:

  • Your business name, address and GSTIN
  • A unique invoice number and the date
  • The customer's name and GSTIN (for B2B sales)
  • Each item's description and HSN/SAC code
  • Quantity, rate and taxable value
  • The GST rate and amount, split into CGST + SGST or IGST
  • The grand total, in figures

CGST, SGST and IGST in one minute

The total GST rate on an item (say 5%, 12% or 18%) is fixed. What changes is how it's split:

  • Within your state — half is CGST (central) and half is SGST (state). A 12% item becomes 6% + 6%.
  • Across states — a single IGST at the full rate is charged instead.

For most kirana and retail counters selling to walk-in customers in the same state, that means CGST + SGST on every line.

Where mistakes creep in — and how software fixes them

Manual GST billing goes wrong in predictable ways: rounding errors, applying the wrong rate to a product, or forgetting to split the tax. Billing software removes all three. You set the correct tax rate once per product, and every bill:

  • picks up the right rate automatically,

  • produces a clean, printable invoice you can hand over or share.

FAQ

What must a GST invoice contain?

Seller name and GSTIN, invoice number and date, buyer details for B2B, item descriptions with HSN/SAC codes, taxable value, the GST rate and amount split into CGST/SGST or IGST, and the total.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

Same-state sales split GST into CGST + SGST; inter-state sales charge a single IGST at the full rate.
