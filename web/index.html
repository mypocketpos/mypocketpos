Frequently asked questions

Does Pocket POS work without internet?

Yes. Pocket POS is offline-first — keep billing with no connection and everything syncs to the cloud automatically once you're back online.

Which businesses is Pocket POS for?

Grocery/kirana, pharmacy, bakery, electronics and garment stores. Pick your business type at sign-up and start with a ready-made product catalog.

Does it support barcode scanning and GST invoices?

Yes. Scan with the camera or any USB/Bluetooth scanner, and generate GST-ready invoices with automatic tax totals.

Can I manage inventory across multiple warehouses?

Yes. Track stock per warehouse, transfer stock between locations, get low-stock alerts, and run multiple POS counters per store.

Is my store data private?

Each store's data is fully isolated in the cloud and only accessible to that store's users after the store is approved.
