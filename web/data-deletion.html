Privacy & Data

Account & Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy and give you complete control over your data.

If you are a shop owner or user of mypocketpos.in and wish to delete your account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. 1

    Send an Email

    Send an email to info@mypocketpos.in

  2. 2

    Use the Correct Subject Line

    Set your email subject to: "Account Deletion Request"

  3. 3

    Provide Your Details

    Include the following information in your email:

    • Registered Store ID
    • Email Address
    • Phone Number

✓ Email Template:

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Store ID: [Your Store ID]
Email: [Your Email]
Phone: [Your Phone Number]

I request to delete my account and associated data.

Data Retention Policy

Upon receiving your request, we will:

  • Delete within 30 days: Your user account details, login credentials, and personal profile data
  • Retain if required: Certain transaction records may be retained for legal and tax compliance where required by law

⚠ Important: Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. After deletion, you will lose access to all your invoices, transactions, and customer data stored in Pocket POS.
